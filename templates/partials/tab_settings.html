Images & local assets

Wiki images may fail because an image filename does not exist or the remote host rejects the request. The app cannot guarantee third-party image availability. Local artwork works across your LAN and does not depend on the wiki.

On the server, run python prepare_assets.py. Fill the generated local_assets folders with your own images. Use Pokédex numbers for Pokémon and item IDs for items, for example pokemon/normal/0025.png and items/1.png. See the generated README for forms and custom filenames.
