How this checklist works

One row per national Pokédex number, with all held forms grouped together. Counts show how many matching Pokémon you currently hold. Expand an owned species to see its exported forms. A Pokémon can fill several categories at once.

XXL and XXS use the export's size classification. Unknown means a held Pokémon has no usable size field, so absence of that size cannot be confirmed. Mega counts currently held Pokémon with an active Mega form, a Mega level, or explicit evolution history.

The national catalog includes species and variants that may not yet be obtainable in Pokémon GO. A dash means no Mega/Gmax form is listed in the bundled catalog. This is a collection checklist, not a release calendar or trading-eligibility guide.
